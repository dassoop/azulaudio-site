Description

Scatter is a powerful cross-platform utility designed to bring organic variation to your sounds by dynamically randomizing pan, volume, and pitch with each triggered note. Inspired by the natural unpredictability of real-world sound design, Scatter ensures that no two playthroughs are ever the same, making it an essential tool for composers, game audio designers, and experimental producers.

With an intuitive interface and deep customization options, Scatter lets you control the range and behavior of each parameter, from subtle fluctuations to extreme transformations. Whether you're crafting immersive soundscapes, randomized percussive textures, or expressive melodic layers, Scatter brings a new level of depth and realism to your projects, all within the power of Kontakt 8.

How you get it
